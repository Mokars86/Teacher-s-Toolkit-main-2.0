Summary: Teacher's ToolKit is an offline-first educational grading, assessment, and classroom management platform created by Mokars Tech. We prioritize teacher privacy and student data protection. We never sell personal or student data to advertisers or data brokers.

1. Introduction

Mokars Tech ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application, Teacher's ToolKit (the "App"), collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information when educators, teachers, and school administrators utilize our software.

By downloading, accessing, or using Teacher's ToolKit, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to provide the educational features of the App:

A. Teacher / User Account Information

  • Account Identifiers: Teacher's name, email address, school affiliation, and optional profile avatar.
  • Authentication: Secure authentication tokens handled via Supabase Auth when teachers choose to link an online account.

B. Classroom & Student Academic Data (Entered by Teachers)

  • Student Records: Student names, identification numbers, and class/grade designations.
  • Academic Performance: Objective test scores, OMR sheet bubble scan results, terminal reports, remarks, and subject marks.
  • Classroom Management: Attendance logs, seating charts, and resource/inventory assignments.

C. Camera & Device Hardware Access

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely to capture and scan physical OMR bubble answer sheets in real-time and for capturing optional profile photos. Images of exam sheets are processed locally on-device and are not sold or exploited.
  • Storage / Media Permissions (READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Used exclusively to export generated PDF grade slips, printable terminal reports, certificates, and to import question banks or answer keys from your local storage.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used strictly for physical haptic confirmation when an OMR sheet is successfully scanned.

D. Billing & Transaction Data

  • When purchasing subscription passes (e.g. End-of-Term passes or Pro upgrades), transactions are processed through Paystack, a certified PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. We do not store your credit card numbers, debit card details, or banking PINs on our servers.

3. How We Use Collected Information

We use the collected information exclusively for the following educational purposes:

  • Calculating exam scores, percentages, and performance trends instantly via our Speed-Ink OMR computer vision engine.
  • Compiling automated terminal report cards, printable grade slips, and certificates.
  • Enabling multi-device synchronization and school-wide collaboration (when opted-in via Cloud Sync).
  • Generating pedagogical lesson plans and curriculum-aligned question banks (via Google Gemini AI API prompts).
  • Validating active workshop vouchers and subscription access.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA, FERPA & GDPR Compliance)

Teacher's ToolKit is an instructional productivity tool designed for educators, teachers, and school administrators.

  • No Direct Child Registration: The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable). Students do not create accounts directly.
  • Educator / School Authority: Any student data (such as names and marks) is entered directly by authorized teachers acting on behalf of the educational institution.
  • No Behavioral Advertising: We never display third-party advertisements to students or use student records for behavioral tracking or commercial marketing.

6. Data Retention, Security & Offline-First Design

Teacher's ToolKit is engineered with an Offline-First philosophy. All grading, exam records, and master keys can function entirely in local device storage without transmitting data over the internet unless the educator explicitly activates Cloud Sync.

When data is synced to the cloud, it is transmitted using modern encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS 1.3) and stored in encrypted databases.

7. User Rights & Data Deletion Requests

Educators have complete control over their stored data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete any individual student record, class test result, or master answer key at any time with a single tap inside the App.
  • Account Deletion: If you wish to delete your entire account, school profile, and all associated cloud data permanently, you may do so inside the App under Settings or by emailing our Data Protection Team at privacy@mokarstech.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
  • All associated data will be permanently wiped from our active databases within 30 days of confirmation.
